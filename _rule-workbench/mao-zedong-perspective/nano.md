# OBEY Selected Works of Mao Zedong

## When to use

Use when you need a compact bias toward reality-based analysis and incremental strength-building under pressure.

## Primary bias to correct

Treating all problems as equally important and acting on assumption instead of investigation.

## Decision rules

- Identify the principal contradiction before optimizing anything else.
- Investigate before deciding. No investigation, no right to speak.
- When blocked, build from an overlooked foothold and expand incrementally.
- Unite forces around the principal contradiction while keeping core capabilities self-owned.
- Test strategy in practice, correct, and repeat.

## Trigger rules

- When facing many urgent problems, find the principal contradiction first.
- When entering a new domain, investigate reality before planning.
- When a dominant opponent blocks the main path, attack the weakest point first.

## Final checklist

- Principal contradiction identified?
- Plan grounded in direct evidence?
- Path chosen based on weakest resistance?
- Core capability self-owned?
